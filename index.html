The short version

Support that meets you where you are

No pressure to perform, to explain yourself, or to be okay. Just people who understand, whenever you need them.

Then

Small, topic-based groups — anxiety, school, family, identity, or simply getting through the week.

Share when you're ready. Read quietly when you're not. Both are completely okay here.

What BEEP Health is

A place to feel less alone — not a replacement for care

Peer support, done safely

Every circle is watched over by trained moderators. Real people keeping conversations kind — not just filters.

Anonymous by design

We never ask for your name, school, or photo. What you share stays between you and the people who choose to listen.

Honest about our limits

BEEP isn't therapy or a crisis line. When you need more, we help you find it — and crisis help stays at the top of every page.

Built with young people

Everything here was shaped by teens and young adults who wanted the space they couldn't find anywhere else.
